Selected Research

HCI · Human-Centered AI · 2025–2026

JobMate

An AI career exploration system grounded in authentic social media peer experiences

How can authentic peer career experiences and LLM-powered personalized dialogue help people understand others' paths and reflect on their own situations?

Approach. Based on 64 survey responses and 8 interviews, we designed and built JobMate, organizing social media career stories into explorable persona cards and source-grounded conversations. We then conducted a between-subjects mixed-methods study with 24 participants, comparing JobMate with directly browsing Xiaohongshu.

Key finding. JobMate helped users connect peer experiences with their own situations, prompting a shift from upward social comparison toward self-reframing and sensemaking; authentic content and context provided emotional and contextual grounding for AI dialogue.

My role: I initiated the project; worked with my advisor to refine the research idea and study design; and led formative interviews, data processing, interaction and system design, full-stack development, user research, data analysis, and paper writing.

Digital Humanities · Comparative Text Analysis · 2023–2024

Computational Textual Linkage between Bai Juyi’s Poetry and Baishi Liutie

How can computational methods help identify and interpret textual connections between classical Chinese texts?

Inspired by manual work by a Chinese literature scholar, I cleaned and organized Bai Juyi's poems and Baishi Liutie, used cosine similarity and string matching to identify textual connections, and compared the computational results with manually identified ones. I then used statistics and visualization to present patterns of association and interpreted them in relation to historical context.

The project shows the potential of automated textual linkage to improve the efficiency of classical-text processing and reveal broader patterns of association.

My role: Under advisor guidance, I independently completed the research design, data processing, method implementation, analysis, visualization, and thesis writing.
